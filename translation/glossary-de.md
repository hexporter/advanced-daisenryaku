# Glossary, German (binding for translation/de)

The German text is translated from the English script (`translation/script`, where the
Japanese original stands next to it); `glossary.md` gives the meaning of each term. Choices
below were made without review (15.11.2026) – change them here first, then in `translation/de`.

## Rules

- Address: "Sie" (briefings: orders to a commander; "Sie haben verloren.").
- Führer directives ("Weisung Nr. …") follow the wording of the German originals where the game
  quotes them; operation names as in German use (Fall Weiß / Gelb / Rot / Blau, Fall
  Barbarossa, Unternehmen Seelöwe / Marita / Taifun / Zitadelle / Wintergewitter / Lüttich,
  Wacht am Rhein, Weserübung). Allied operations keep their names ("Operation Torch").
- Place names in German (Warschau, Weichsel, Warthe, Kiew, Charkow, Rostow, Sewastopol, Arnheim,
  Maas, Tarent, Dünkirchen); Piotrkow stays.
- 16x16 text has ä ö ü ß. The **8x8 names have none** (the game font): ae oe ue ss, or an
  abbreviation that avoids them (Daenem., Tuerkei, Suedfrt., Weissr.F, Oedland, Wueste).
- The title menu (CONTINUE, CAMPAIGN, STANDARD, CAMPAIGN START 1939), NOW / GREAT / END on the
  turn screen and REAL / ON / OFF are Latin in the original and stay as they are. "vs" and "%"
  stay.
