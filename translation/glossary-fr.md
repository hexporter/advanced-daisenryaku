# Glossary, French (binding for translation/fr)

The French text is translated from the English script (`translation/script`, where the
Japanese original stands next to it); `glossary.md` gives the meaning of each term. Choices
below were made without review (15.11.2026) – change them here first, then in `translation/fr`.

## Rules

- Address: "vous" (briefings: orders to a commander; "Vous avez perdu.").
- Führer directives: "Directive n° …"; operation names as in French use (Plan Blanc / Jaune /
  Rouge / Bleu, Opération Barbarossa / Lion de mer / Marita / Typhon / Citadelle / Tempête
  d'hiver / Lüttich, Garde sur le Rhin, Weserübung). Allied operations keep their names
  ("opération Torch"). "Führer", "Wehrmacht", "Afrikakorps" stay.
- Place names in French (Varsovie, Vistule, Moscou, Léningrad, Kharkov, Sébastopol, Koursk,
  Anvers, Meuse, Tarente, Dunkerque); Piotrkow, Arnhem stay.
- Quotations that are French in the original are given in the original (de Gaulle, 18 June;
  Verlaine's line for Overlord).
- Space before ":" "?" ";" as in French typography; quotation marks are the straight ones of
  the font.
- 16x16 text has the accented letters and œ. The **8x8 names have none** (the game font):
  plain letters (Norvege, Grece, Foret, Riviere).
- The title menu, NOW / GREAT / END on the turn screen and REAL / ON / OFF are Latin in the
  original and stay as they are. "vs" and "%" stay.
