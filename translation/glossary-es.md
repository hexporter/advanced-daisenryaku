# Glossary, Spanish (binding for translation/es)

The Spanish text is translated from the English script (`translation/script`, where the
Japanese original stands next to it); `glossary.md` gives the meaning of each term. Choices
below were made without review (15.11.2026) – change them here first, then in `translation/es`.

## Rules

- Address: "usted" (briefings: orders to a commander; "Ha perdido."). European Spanish
  (Rumanía, carro for tank, contracarro).
- Führer directives: "Directiva n.º …"; operation names as in Spanish use (Caso Blanco /
  Amarillo / Rojo / Azul, Operación Barbarroja / León Marino / Marita / Tifón / Ciudadela /
  Tormenta de Invierno / Lüttich, Guardia en el Rin, Weserübung). Allied operations keep their
  names ("Operación Torch"). "Führer", "Wehrmacht", "Afrika Korps" stay.
- Place names in Spanish (Varsovia, Vístula, Moscú, Leningrado, Járkov, Sebastopol, Amberes,
  Mosa, Tarento, Bastoña); Piotrkow, Arnhem, Dunkerque stay.
- 16x16 text has the accented letters, ñ, ¿ and ¡. The **8x8 names have none** (the game
  font): plain letters, and words with ñ are avoided (Monte, Can for cañón, "Roto" for dañado).
- The title menu, NOW / GREAT / END on the turn screen and REAL / ON / OFF are Latin in the
  original and stay as they are. "vs" and "%" stay.
